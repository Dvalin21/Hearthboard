HearthBoard is a privacy-first, open-source family calendar and task manager for Android 8+.

Features:

• Calendar views: Month, Week, Day, Agenda — adaptive to any screen size
• CalDAV sync: Google Calendar, Fastmail, Nextcloud, iCloud, any CalDAV server
• Delta sync: CTag/ETag-based — only fetches what changed
• Tasks (VTODO): full task management with priority, assignment, and CalDAV sync
• Star rewards: motivate kids by assigning stars to chores and tasks
• Color-coded checklists: shopping, to-do, grocery lists
• Meal planner: weekly breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack grid per person
• People: per-person color coding, role management (parent/child/dependent), and task assignment
• Countdown timers for birthdays, vacations, and special events
• Material 3: light + dark mode, seed color theming
• Adaptive layout: bottom nav on phones, nav rail on tablets (5.5"–14.5")
• Kiosk mode: optional PIN to lock the app as the device launcher
• AES-256/GCM encryption: CalDAV passwords encrypted with Android Keystore

Privacy & Security:

• Zero telemetry: no analytics SDKs, no Firebase, no crash reporters
• No Google Play Services dependency — works on de-Googled devices
• The only network requests are HTTP calls to your CalDAV server
• Every permission has a documented purpose — no unnecessary permissions
• F-Droid compatible: single universal APK, no proprietary dependencies

Hearthboard is GPL-3.0 licensed. No subscriptions, no tracking, no account required.
