Photo sync: the cursor no longer resets after every run, so syncs stay incremental instead of re-downloading everything.

CalDAV: local edits are uploaded, a failed server query no longer wipes cached events, and a renamed collection no longer deletes the fresh row.

Backup restore is atomic now, so a corrupt backup cannot empty the app.

Photo sync closes its connections and never reports a missing file as uploaded.
